# Glossary

Beginner-friendly definitions for common market technology terms used in MarketX.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Stock | A small ownership share in a company. If you own a stock, you own a tiny piece of that company. |
| Exchange | A marketplace where buyers and sellers meet electronically to trade instruments like stocks. |
| Broker | A firm that helps clients access markets and send orders to exchanges. |
| Trader | A person or automated system that decides to buy or sell financial instruments. |
| Bid | The price a buyer is willing to pay. The best bid is the highest current buy price. |
| Ask | The price a seller is willing to accept. The best ask is the lowest current sell price. |
| Spread | The difference between the best ask and the best bid. A smaller spread usually means trading is easier and cheaper. |
| Order Book | A live list of buy and sell orders waiting at different prices. |
| Market Order | An order to buy or sell immediately at the best available price. It prioritizes speed over price certainty. |
| Limit Order | An order to buy or sell at a specific price or better. It gives price control but may not execute immediately. |
| Liquidity | How easy it is to buy or sell without moving the price too much. More orders usually mean more liquidity. |
| Execution | The moment an order, or part of an order, successfully trades. |
| Settlement | The process where the buyer receives the asset and the seller receives the cash. |
| Clearing | The process of confirming trade details and managing obligations before settlement. |
| OMS | Order Management System. A system that receives, tracks, routes, and updates orders throughout their lifecycle. |
| Risk Engine | A system that checks whether an order is allowed before it reaches the market. |
| Matching Engine | The exchange system that matches compatible buy and sell orders. |
| Position | The quantity of an instrument owned or owed by an account, trader, or firm. |
| PnL | Profit and Loss. A measure of money made or lost from trading activity. |
| FIX Protocol | Financial Information eXchange. A common messaging protocol used by trading systems to send orders and execution reports. |
| Market Data | Real-time or delayed information about prices, trades, order books, and other market activity. |
